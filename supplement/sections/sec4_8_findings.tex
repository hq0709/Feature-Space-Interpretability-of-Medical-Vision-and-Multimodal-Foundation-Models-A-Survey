Table~\ref{tab:synthesis} is the whole of this section in five lines: what the corpus reports, what it
stops short of, what limits each conclusion and the experiment that would settle it. The rest of the
section is the evidence behind those five, statement by statement. Table~\ref{tab:statements} lists the
\Fnrows{} evidence statements in three classes, so that breadth is not read as strength: \Fnmulti{}
reported across multiple studies, \Fnsingle{} single-study or single-group signals and \Fncond{}
conditional or descriptive inventories, the last being rows conditional on the selection defining them
(F7 selects the dictionaries that attempted validation; F9b, F10 and F11 list heterogeneous records).
Every statement describes this corpus; none is a claim about the size or composition of the literature.
A row is a proposition that the finding statements of at least one record assert, split where records
assert strictly different claims (F1b, F1b$'$ and F1b$''$ separate held-out recovery, gradient
association and labelled correspondence; F8a, F8a$'$ and F8a$''$ separate an instrumented failure, a
disagreement and an implausibility); of the \Nclaims{} records \Fnorow{} support no row and \Fmultirow{} are counted in more than one, \Fmultithree{} of them in three. The synthesis is descriptive: where records disagree, the disagreement is stated rather than averaged, and no direction of
effect is counted across records.

Three conventions govern every number. A record is one study $\times$ family $\times$ evidence type
$\times$ locus (\S\ref{sec:method}) and can bundle several concepts, models, datasets or end-points, so a
validity test recorded on it need not cover every outcome inside it; Table~\ref{tab:vcov} and
Fig.~\ref{fig:findings} print the strict all-outcome count and the any-outcome bound wherever the two
differ. Counts are taken on all \Nclaims{} records. \Fflagrec{} of them, from \Fdefstud{} studies, carry a recorded defect of one of five result-invalidating kinds (resubstitution, leakage across split units, circular estimation, selection on
the reported metric or single-case fitting, the first two being the commonest leakage errors of
machine-learning-based science~\cite{kapoor2023leakage}) and are marked wherever they appear rather than removed. And the counts weight neither sample size nor effect size, so they estimate neither prevalence
nor certainty; datasets and lineages recur, CheXpert, MIMIC-CXR and TCGA running through F1a, F2, F2b and
F3a alone, and only \Nminvalid{} of the \Ncore{} studies state a patient-, slide- or subject-level split,
evaluate on data unused for fitting and report seeds, intervals or tests. Breadth is therefore reported as
a description of this corpus and not as a weight, and the conclusions of Table~\ref{tab:synthesis} rest on
the corpus as reported, not on a validated subset of it.

How well a row's evidence is safeguarded is a separate question from how much of it there is, and no
grade combines the two. Table~\ref{tab:vcov} pairs each of the \Vnclasses{} test classes with the
statement it licenses beyond the base reading its design already supports (held-out performance above
the study-stated reference) and with what it does not license. A test validates the measurement, not the
proposition, so two tested records are not a replication of the finding, and a share is comparable within
a class, never across classes. On these counts the corpus is thinner than any pooled figure suggests.
Capacity and representation-attribution controls on probes are rare, a permutation null appearing in
\Vperm{} of \Vpermn{} records and a randomly initialised encoder in \Vlrn{} of \Vlrnn{}, and the
randomized control task of Hewitt and Liang~\cite{hewitt2019designing} is reported in no record (its
construction needs repeated input types that medical images seldom have, so no applicability denominator
is given, \S\ref{sec:future}). \Vspca{}--\Vspc{} of \Vspcn{} targeted interventions report a matched
control (the strict all-outcome count and the any-outcome bound), and a comparison model appears in
\Vcomp{} of \Vcompn{} geometry records.

The evidence behind the five conclusions of Table~\ref{tab:synthesis} reads as follows.

\emph{Availability against use.} The decodability findings (F1a, F1a$^{\prime}$, F1b, F1c, F2b) rest on
18, 15, 6, 12 and 11 distinct first authors over all their records and on four or more modalities each.
The four findings of most practical consequence rest on far less. Linear redundancy of a decodable
attribute for a refitted readout (F4) rests on one record; attributions failing an instrumented
faithfulness test (F8a) on six studies, with method disagreement (F8a$'$) and
qualitative implausibility (F8a$''$) carried as separate, weaker rows; and a targeted intervention
exceeding a matched control (F9a) on three records, only one against random directions. The
interventional record is too heterogeneous and too weakly controlled to support a magnitude claim: the
eight records to which a matched-direction control applies use incomparable end-points, none reports a
dose--response test in the sense used here (at least three pre-specified magnitudes on the same held-out
end-point, reported as a curve), and two vary a magnitude while tuning it~\cite{chen2026vihd,nooralahzadeh2026universal},
which selects an operating point rather than characterising a response. The effects can be listed but
not compared, from under 0.02 AUROC for a post-hoc gap shift~\cite{restrepo2026cone} and under 0.001 for
residualising a demographic attribute~\cite{kim2026encoding}$^{\dagger}$ to the collapse of a finding's
own AUROC when the roughly ten channels that decode it are zeroed~\cite{nooralahzadeh2026sparse}
Post-hoc operations on frozen embeddings (F3b, F5, F6,
F9b) are the only interventions with more than a handful of records.

\emph{Acquisition and site structure.} Centre, scanner and device are recoverable from pathology and
brain-MRI embeddings in every record of F2b and F3c and in all but one of F3a's, by 11, 16 and 3
distinct first authors, and centre rivals or exceeds biological structure where both are measured. The benefit of removing it is operation-specific: among the records of F3b, affine harmonisation raises cross-scanner retrieval
and centre removal raises a robustness index without improving downstream generalisation, and no record
reports the downstream end-point after harmonisation on a held-out site.

\emph{Meaning of discovered features.} Naming and validation are separate steps, and an automatically
named feature can still be validated. The \Nsaeval{} dictionaries with a reported semantic check share one property, the locus: all read encoder-side pooled, tile or patch representations, and none of the three
trained on decoder residual streams is checked against clinicians or structured labels. The two sides
also report different feature types, which we state as a hypothesis, that residual-stream dictionaries of report generators surface report-level rather than image-level concepts, confounded by model, layer,
dictionary design and naming, and testable by the matched design of \S\ref{sec:future}. Of the \Nsaeval{} with a
clinician- or label-based check, \Nsaemanual{} are named manually, \Nsaelabmatch{} by label matching,
\Nsaellm{} by a language model and \Nsaetext{} by text matching, so an automatic naming route does not
prevent validation and a manual one does not supply it. \Nsaestab{} of the \Nsae{} report any
reproducibility check, and none establishes cross-seed identity of its features.

\emph{Medical against general pre-training.} \Nmvgrec{} core records from \Nmvgstud{} studies compare a
medical with a general foundation model on the same data; seventeen further
records compare only with an ImageNet- or task-supervised classifier and are excluded. F10 collects the
decoding records and F11 those in which a property is shared or differs; three records are marked not
compared, because the general model is measured only on zero-shot accuracy rather than on the coded
property, and three with a determinate outcome fall outside both rows. Where the medical model is better,
it is better by degree: under linear probing on chest radiographs~\cite{li2025featurequality}, for a breast
image--text encoder over CLIP and DINOv2 by 9.7 and 13.3\% relative F1~\cite{germani2025bias}, under
frozen-feature adaptation in retina~\cite{zhou2025generalist}, in expert concept scoring in
dermatology~\cite{kim2024transparent,song2024morphological}, and in pathology segmentation and cell-type
or gene-expression decoding against a ResNet-50~\cite{gildenblat2024segmentation,cui2026translating}.
Against this, general self-supervised features match medical baselines across more than 200 radiology
evaluations~\cite{baharoon2023dinov2}, a specialist model beats every foundation model in large-scale
radiological retrieval~\cite{denner2024leveraging}, no encoder dominates ten three-dimensional CT
models~\cite{chevli2026frozenct}, cohort-specific models learn complementary rather than better pathology
features~\cite{neidlinger2025benchmark}, and a general encoder is at least as good after post-hoc
alignment~\cite{folco2025semantic,wesp2026sparse}. The properties that matter most for interpretability
are shared: effective dimensionality is a small fraction of the nominal dimension in seven of eight
encoders, four medical and four general, and whitening degrades all eight~\cite{garciahidalgo2026embedding};
shortcut reliance appears in both on the same radiographs~\cite{li2025featurequality,sourget2025fairness};
both lose small-lesion signal at global pooling~\cite{muthyala2026frozen}; subgroup disparities hold
whichever of twenty models is used~\cite{jin2024fairmedfm}; medical dual encoders show the modality gap of
their general counterparts~\cite{restrepo2026cone}; and images whose nearest neighbours carry the opposite
label overlap above chance far more among medical encoders than in two natural-image
tasks~\cite{arasteh2026candor}. These are convenience comparisons that vary architecture, objective, scale
and data at once; the one study that stratifies the objective in a controlled matrix finds convergence
driven by the objective rather than by clinical supervision, but does not vary medical against general
pre-training~\cite{tayebiarasteh2026self}. The effect of medical pre-training itself is therefore
unidentified, and no study tests whether an interpretability method transfers between the two kinds of
model (\S\ref{sec:future}).

\emph{Where to measure.} Three studies that vary the readout within one model suggest, as a hypothesis
rather than a row, that readout granularity can dominate apparent decodability for small or spatially
localised targets: pooled probes sit at chance where patch-local probes on the same forward pass reach
0.899 AUC, but only under an oracle lesion box the authors call undeployable~\cite{muthyala2026frozen};
shallow blocks carry spatial information better than deep ones~\cite{vadori2025mind}; and acquisition
site occupies a large principal component~\cite{rahbar2026frozen}. They vary different things and no
design separates model, depth and pooling, so what follows for practice is only that an availability
claim should name its sublocus as well as its model. Beyond the encoder, class labels become less
decodable after the connector in one uncontrolled successive-locus probe (F8b), and no other core record
measures a medical connector or the text encoder as a locus of its own.
