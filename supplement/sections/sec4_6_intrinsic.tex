\subsection{Intrinsic and concept-aligned designs built on foundation models}
\label{sec:intrinsic}

Intrinsic designs constrain part of a representation to be interpretable by construction, a model
property; analysis stays in the preceding families. Eligible designs carry the intrinsic-design tag and are
coded in the supporting family, decoding except for one discovery-coded prototype design.

\textbf{Concept bottlenecks on frozen medical encoders.} Concept bottleneck models (CBMs) predict
human-defined concepts, then labels from concepts alone, $\hat c=g_c(z_\lambda(x))$ and $\hat y=g_y(\hat c)$,
so labels depend on the representation only through inspectable $\hat c$~\cite{koh2020concept}; concept
embedding models relax the bottleneck to a vector per concept to recover accuracy~\cite{zarlenga2022concept}.
Post-hoc and label-free variants attach to pre-trained encoders, language models proposing and vision--language models
scoring concepts~\cite{yuksekgonul2022post,oikarinen2023label,yang2022language}. GPT-4 concepts scored by
a vision--language model give classifiers across eight medical datasets~\cite{yan2023robust}, textbook-
and PubMed-derived concept spaces degrade less than black-box fine-tuning on constructed confound-reversed
splits, a shortcut test rather than a natural external shift~\cite{yang2024textbook}, and stronger
intermediate- than final-layer associations motivate multi-layer bottlenecks~\cite{wang2025mvp}.
Applications reach volumetric, slide and ultrasound
encoders~\cite{rojas2026echopcbm,tushar2026distilling,gao2025evaluating,sun2025label}: frozen-CT ridge
concept heads recover lung-nodule attributes ($R^2\le 0.24$) yet generalise externally better than
embedding probes, and pathology-encoder graph heads feed thirty kidney-cancer concepts to a survival
model. Distribution shift degrades frozen-feature bottlenecks, partly recovered by concept-layer
adaptation~\cite{choi2024adaptive}; VLM-supervised concepts can differ from expert annotations, and
concept accuracy relates weakly to quality~\cite{debole2025if} (\S\ref{sec:eval-what}).

\textbf{Concept-based vision--language models and report-grounded bottlenecks.} Here the concept layer
sits inside a medical VLM, where radiologist-style observation probabilities yield zero-shot
diagnoses~\cite{pellegrini2023xplainer}. Elicited criteria become textual anchors in a VLM's joint
space~\cite{gao2024aligning} or explainable prompts~\cite{bie2024xcoop}, and large VLMs classify from
their own answers to concept questions, a contextual output-level procedure~\cite{patricio2025cbvlm}; a two-step
variant reads dermoscopic concepts off frozen MONET image--text similarity and passes them to a language model,
beating a trained bottleneck on three datasets, with ground-truth concepts adding 5--21 points and BiomedCLIP concepts
at chance~\cite{patricio2025twostep}. Report-derived concepts
in a chest-radiograph joint embedding provide counterfactuals~\cite{sadashivaiah2024explaining}, and
guideline text supports conformant classification in a general CLIP model~\cite{harmanani2026vision}. A radiology foundation model projects chest radiographs into
language-model concept space, trained on more than 0.87M image--report pairs and validated on
physician-annotated cohorts~\cite{han2026clear}.

\textbf{Prototype and part-based models on foundation features.} Case- and part-prototype models explain by
similarity to exemplars~\cite{chen2019thislooks,barnett2021case,kim2021xprotonet}; foundation-model versions
summarise slide patch embeddings into morphological prototypes~\cite{song2024morphological} or
give patch-level prototypes interactive concept meaning~\cite{huy2025interactive}, a self-interpretable
multiple-instance model pairs handcrafted and deep branches~\cite{kapse2023si}, region-additive logistic heads on
frozen BrainIAC and NeuroFM embeddings of anatomically masked volumes match fine-tuning while randomised
embeddings fall to chance~\cite{zhang2026regionfm}, and
attribute-regularised or concept-whitened spaces align axes with clinical
attributes~\cite{cetin2022attri,hou2024concept}, the medical instances of concept whitening~\cite{chen2020concept}. Being feature-space
points and directions, they are evaluated by decoding and discovery.

\textbf{Critiques.} Bottleneck intervention requires labels to depend only on concepts, yet predictors may
ignore relevant regions~\cite{margeloiu2021do} and soft scores leak non-concept information~\cite{havasi2022addressing}. Random concept projections match learned accuracy, so
accuracy cannot certify meaning~\cite{srivastava2024vlg,schmalwasser2026faithfulness};
information-theoretic measures quantify leakage~\cite{parisini2025leakage,makonnen2025measuring}, and
intervenability extends beyond bottlenecks~\cite{marcinkevics2024beyond}. Inconsistent dermoscopy annotations cap
hard-bottleneck accuracy~\cite{napoles2026concept}, and concept accuracy can
conceal a meaningless layer~\cite{han2026loss}. A sparse autoencoder before a linear
bottleneck~\cite{rao2024discover} inherits discovery instability
(\S\ref{sec:discovery}).


\subsubsection{Summary and discussion}

Intrinsic designs make concept layers auditable and intervenable at task accuracy competitive with the same
backbone without the concept layer, where that comparison is reported, but
faithfulness requires valid concepts and controlled leakage, which accuracy cannot show. \Vintrany{} of the
\Vintrn{} intrinsic-design records report a control on the concept layer (Table~\ref{tab:vcov}), an embedding-randomisation
null~\cite{zhang2026regionfm} and the replacement of predicted concepts by ground
truth~\cite{patricio2025twostep}; comparisons of the same frozen encoder with and without an intrinsic
head, and interventions on $\hat c$ with an expert-validated $g_c$ and measured leakage, are rarely
reported together.
