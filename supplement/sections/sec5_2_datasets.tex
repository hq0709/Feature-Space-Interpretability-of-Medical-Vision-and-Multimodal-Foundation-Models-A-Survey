Table~\ref{tab:datasets} lists the recurring resources; three properties of them bound what
representation-level evaluation can currently establish.

\textbf{Label provenance.} The large chest-radiograph corpora carry findings extracted from reports by
automatic labellers rather than assigned by
radiologists~\cite{irvin2019chexpert,johnson2019mimic,johnson2019mimicjpg,wang2017chestxray}. A probe
fitted to them measures agreement with a labeller's reading of the report, not with a radiologist reading
the image. Expert resources supply a direct clinical
reference~\cite{nguyen2020vindr,liu2020chestxdet,bustos2019padchest,jain2021radgraph,smit2020chexbert}.
In pathology the two resources listed differ in kind, a text corpus and expert region
annotations~\cite{ikezogwo2023quilt,litjens2018camelyon}, and concept-scored image--text models are
resources in their own right~\cite{kim2024transparent,nie2025explainable}. Volumetric imaging offers
report-derived abnormality labels only~\cite{hamamci2024ctrate,draelos2020radchestct}.

\textbf{Expert concept density.} Dermatology is the best-annotated modality, with checklist criteria,
dermatologist-assigned concepts, dermoscopic structure masks and a hierarchically annotated South-Indian
cohort~\cite{kawahara2019derm7pt,daneshjou2023skincon,mendonca2013ph2,codella2019isic,madarkar2025dermacon};
and it therefore carries the best-validated concept bottlenecks. Even here the annotation is not a ground
truth: 16\% of Derm7pt concept profiles map to conflicting diagnoses~\cite{napoles2026concept}.

\textbf{Demographic metadata.} Confounder analysis needs attributes the datasets rarely record: only two of
23 audited public chest-radiograph datasets report ethnicity~\cite{yi2022demographic}. EMBED supplies
racially balanced mammography~\cite{jeong2022embed} and FairMedFM combines 17 datasets with sensitive
attributes across 20 foundation models~\cite{jin2024fairmedfm}.
