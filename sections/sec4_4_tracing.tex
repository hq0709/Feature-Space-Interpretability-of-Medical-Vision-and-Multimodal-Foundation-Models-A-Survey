\subsection{Representation attribution, localisation and tracing}
\label{sec:tracing}

Tracing observes forward passes without changing computation, localising concepts to layers,
positions, heads or training examples, each a hypothesis for later patching or steering; the medical
studies ask where multimodal LLMs use images.

\subsubsection{Residual-stream decomposition and lenses}

The logit lens reads an intermediate state through the unembedding matrix,
$\mathrm{softmax}(W_U\,\mathrm{LN}(h^{(\ell)}_p))$; the tuned lens learns a layer-specific affine
map~\cite{belrose2023eliciting}. In vision--language models visual tokens become text-decodable and
middle layers enrich then refine, while visual information declines across layers and generation
steps~\cite{neo2024towards,jiang2024devils,li2025the}; objects are named without fine-grained
comparison and steering vectors leave no lens-decodable
trace~\cite{shahgir2026vlms,nadaf2026steerable}. Medical lenses track diagnostic formation in a
breast-cancer transformer~\cite{takahashi2026vision}, place multilingual knowledge at intermediate
layers but not at output~\cite{abouzahir2026bridging}, and find early visual attributes regardless
of answer correctness~\cite{nooralahzadeh2026arbitration}.

\subsubsection{Attention, visual tokens and the connector}

Attention to the image is measured as visual-token mass $\sum_{q\in\mathcal V}A^{(\ell,h)}_{pq}$
over heads and layers, or by rollout and its
refinements~\cite{abnar2020quantifying,chefer2021transformer}; none quantifies causal contribution,
as the attention-as-explanation debate
established~\cite{jain2019attention,wiegreffe2019attention,serrano2019is}. General-domain work finds
middle-layer concentration on real objects, staged flow into question tokens, attention sinks,
redundant visual tokens and modality-specific
neurons~\cite{jiang2024devils,zhang2024cross,kang2025you,luo2025sink,chen2024image,wang2025token,huo2024mmneuron,huang2024miner,kang2025large,kim2025interpreting,jiang2025investigating}.
Connectors map correlated embeddings into language-model feature
space~\cite{lee2026more,venhoff2025visual} and hallucinate when
text-over-aligned~\cite{fan2026visual,venhoff2025too,saini2026when}; dermatology fine-tuning
improves the projection but not its domain specificity~\cite{verma2024cross}.

Eleven core records from eleven studies, nine of which take tracing as their primary family (\S\ref{sec:method}), read attention or attribution maps of medical models; \Vtrany{} test them against
perturbation, occlusion or counterfactual evidence, and where tested against a perturbation or counterfactual, the maps often fail, and the result is benchmark- and end-point-dependent. Four attribution methods disagree under patch deletion and insertion across 17 RETFound
models, gradient-weighted rollout winning in 11 of 17~\cite{bors2026benchmarking}, and internal
attributions of six vision--language models miss the counterfactually verified evidence region on
chest radiographs (rollout IoU 2.7 against 39.2 for the best gradient
method)~\cite{xiong2026rethinking}. Attention maps sometimes anti-correlate with occlusion
importance~\cite{sadanandan2026attention}, lose to perturbation and relevance-propagation
explanations~\cite{idaji2026beyond}, enrich for pathway rather than gene
signatures~\cite{srikanthan2026do}, mark pixels outside the body~\cite{hashmi2024towards} and are
diffuse and prompt-insensitive~\cite{injarabian2025interpreting}, while an EchoPrime probe beats
random masking only by a method-dependent margin~\cite{akanova2026interpreting}$^{\dagger}$.

The remaining records localise rather than test: visual-attention ratios find a visually dominant
window in two medical multimodal LLMs (\S\ref{sec:intervention})~\cite{chen2026vihd}, and
class-label probes at successive loci of fourteen models name four failure modes including connector
fidelity loss~\cite{zhu2026lost}. The earliest record audits GLoRIA's text--image attention on Chest
ImaGenome pairs: the map localises above chance (AUROC 69.1) yet barely moves when ``left'' becomes
``right'' and falls only when the reference boxes move, so it is fixed by the
image~\cite{mcinerney2022that}. Text-tower anchors trace negation failure in a chest-radiograph
assistant and in radiology captions~\cite{vu2025improving,abbasi2026layerspecific}; input audits
agree that image use is weak, a text-only baseline nearly matching the best multimodal
model~\cite{lotfinia2026vision}, VQA grounding staying poor~\cite{chen2026grounding} and grounding
failures separating~\cite{liu2026medical}. No other study measures a medical connector
(Fig.~\ref{fig:corpus}b), so these token-level results transfer only where projectors preserve patch
correspondence (Table~\ref{tab:models}).

\subsubsection{Encoder tokens, attention heads and slide-level aggregators}

Three observations bear on the encoders of Table~\ref{tab:models}, none a core claim. The high-norm
artefact tokens of vision transformers~\cite{darcet2023vision} are unmeasured in DINOv2-recipe
models such as RAD-DINO, UNI and Prov-GigaPath, where they would dominate the patch norms and
attention probes read. Self-supervised pathology encoders develop heads attending to morphological
phenotypes~\cite{chen2022self,chen2024generalpurpose,xu2024wholeslide}, qualitative evidence that
morphology was learned. Tile weights of attention-based multiple-instance learning (MIL)
aggregators~\cite{ilse2018attention,lu2020data} are the common computational-pathology explanation,
partly answered by prototype- and concept-based aggregators
(\S\ref{sec:intrinsic})~\cite{song2024morphological,huy2025interactive}. Like decoder attention,
none is confirmed by patching.

\subsubsection{Example- and training-data-based attribution}

Frozen embeddings support content-based retrieval over 1.6 million radiological images~\cite{denner2024leveraging}, and foundation-feature prototypes (\S\ref{sec:intrinsic})
turn nearest-neighbour structure into explanation. Text-based decomposition attributes CLIP
representations to layers, heads and
patches~\cite{gandelsman2023interpreting,balasubramanian2024decomposing}, with no medical instance.
Influence functions~\cite{koh2017influence} trace predictions to training examples, identifying mislabelled
data~\cite{hao2020inaccurate} and re-weighting noisy labels~\cite{braun2022influence}, and scalable
gradient attribution has reached a biomedical language model~\cite{cao2026scalable}; medical vision
use stays limited to curation and leakage auditing (\S\ref{sec:applications}), where image--report
correspondence creates a re-linkage risk~\cite{tayebiarasteh2026crossmodal}.


\subsubsection{Summary and discussion}

Tracing localises information and shows how unevenly it is attended, not that the output uses it: in
language models the layer that causal tracing singles out is not the layer at which editing works
best~\cite{hase2023does}. The medical records that test their maps find them unreliable or
method-dependent, and image use weak. \Vtrany{} of the \Vtrn{} records
\emph{test} their map, by occlusion, perturbation, patching or counterfactual evidence, and the test
corroborates the map in some and refutes it in others; localisation itself remains a hypothesis, and artefact tokens threaten the
patch-level analyses reviewed here.
